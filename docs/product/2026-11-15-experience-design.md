# Experience design — making every invitation fun

Date: 2026-11-15. Status: **draft for approval**. Companion to `2026-11-15-product-plan.md`.
Evidence: [V] read on the vendor's page, [S] search snippet only. Rankings and build ratings are our own judgement — no source ranks these mechanics by popularity.

**Decided by you:** the guest experience must be genuinely entertaining (teams, games, competition); launch sets are weddings, gender reveal and corporate events (catered gatherings were considered and dropped on 2026-11-15); brand is Arabic with Arabic and English at equal weight; pricing as proposed; name and palette are placeholders until you send the real ones.

---

> **Scope note (2026-11-15):** launch contents are decided in `2026-11-15-launch-scope.md`. Where the priorities below differ, that document wins.

## 1. What the research says

- **Nobody in Arabic does guest games.** Arabic results were only freelancers selling animated invitation pages. Arabic QR-invitation apps have no game layer.
- **The closest products abroad:**
  - QuizYourGuests [V] — Team Bride vs Team Groom, points for quiz answers, photo uploads and meeting someone from the other side; big-screen mode. Free with banner / £29.
  - RevealTogether [V] — Team Boy / Team Girl live tally, synchronised countdown on every screen, a "secret keeper" who enters the answer so the parents are surprised too. $7.99–$39.99.
  - genderreveal.live [V] — five reveal styles (countdown, balloon pop, mystery box, wheel, scratch card), share video for stories.
  - Trivia Anywhere [V] — teams ranked by average score so a small table can win.
  - Goosechase [V] — photo/GPS missions with live feed and leaderboard. $99 per event for 10 participants.
  - Kahoot / Slido / Mentimeter [V] — live quiz, polls, Q&A upvoting, word clouds; all need a presenter.
- **What they all share:** no app, no account, join with just a name, optional big-screen mode.
- **What none of them do:** start before the event, live inside the invitation itself, work WhatsApp-first, speak Arabic, or carry on after the event.

That last line is our opening: the games are not a separate tool the host has to set up — they are part of the invitation link, from the first open to the recap.

## 2. The play engine (built once, used by every occasion)

Every occasion is a content pack on top of the same parts.

| Part | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Join** | Guest opens their link, already recognised by name. No account, no app. |
| **Teams** | Pick or get assigned a side. Roster shows who is on each team. Live score bar. |
| **Points** | Small actions score: RSVP, quiz, prediction, photo, check-in. Team score uses the average, so the bigger family or department does not win automatically. |
| **Mini-games** | Quiz, this-or-that vote, predictions, bingo card, photo missions, tap race, guess-the-photo, polls. Each is a reusable module with a question bank per occasion. |
| **Live room** | Host triggers a moment (reveal, quiz round, draw). Everyone's screen changes at the same second. Who's online, reactions. |
| **Big screen** | Optional view for the hall projector: scores, photo wall, live results. Everything also works without it. |
| **Share cards** | After every action, a personal image (team badge, score, winner card) sized for WhatsApp groups and stories, with the invitation link inside. Names and scores only — never photos. |
| **Timeline** | Things unlock on a schedule: teasers before, live rounds during, results and recap after. A reason to reopen the link. |
| **Recap** | Final scores, winners, best photos, personal result. Works without login. |
| **Host controls** | Turn any game on or off, pick questions from a vetted bank or write their own, moderate uploads, choose what guests can see of each other. |

Rules across all sets:
- Every game works self-paced; live rounds are a bonus, not a requirement.
- Default to team scores; individual rankings are opt-in.
- Uploads are moderated and private by default.
- Nothing depends on hall Wi-Fi or a projector.

## 3. Weddings (engagement, katb kitab, henna, wedding)

**Core loop:** أهل العريس vs أهل العروسة (plus a friends team), playing from the day the link arrives until the recap.

| # | Mechanic | What the guest does | When | Release |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pick your side | Chooses groom's side, bride's side or friends; gets a badge; sees the roster and score | Before → after | Launch |
| 2 | "Who knows the couple best" quiz | 8–10 timed questions; score goes to the team | Before + live round | Launch |
| 3 | Predictions | Who cries first, how late the zaffa starts, first-dance song; settled on the day | Before → during | Launch |
| 4 | مين فيهم؟ (shoe game, guest edition) | Votes him/her on each question, then sees the room vs. the couple's real answer | Before or live | Launch |
| 5 | Guess the baby photo | Matches childhood photos to bride, groom or family | Before | Launch |
| 6 | Song requests with upvotes | Suggests and upvotes; top list goes to the DJ | Before + during | Launch |
| 7 | Voice / video guestbook | Records 30 seconds for the couple | During + after | Launch |
| 8 | Photo missions + live photo wall | Uploads against prompts ("selfie with someone from the other side"); appears on the hall screen | During | Release 2 |
| 9 | Cross-side icebreaker | Swaps codes with someone from the other family; both score | During | Release 2 |
| 10 | Wedding bingo | Shuffled card ("a zaghrouta", "uncle dances"); taps as they happen | During | Release 2 |
| 11 | Zaffa tap race / zaghrouta meter | Host triggers "الزفة بدأت"; everyone taps the tabla rhythm; team energy bars on screen | During, live | Release 2 |
| 12 | Daily countdown question | One question a day in the last week | Before | Release 2 |
| 13 | Advice & du'a jar | Leaves advice or a blessing for the couple | Before + after | Launch |
| 14 | Guest awards | Votes best dressed, best dancer; winners announced | During | Release 2 |
| 15 | Couple's choices polls | Votes on honeymoon activity, cake flavour | Before | Launch |

**Modes:**
- **Henna night:** women-only link, private photo wall, outfit vote ("أنهي لوك أحلى؟"), "guess the next song".
- **Katb el kitab:** calm — blessings wall and predictions only; no loud games around the contract.

**Avoid:** showing no2ta amounts or ranking givers; public photos by default; alcohol or kiss-themed mechanics copied from Western products (the toast becomes sharbat); embarrassing couple questions — we ship a vetted Arabic and English question bank; rivalry copy that insults a family.

## 4. Gender reveal

**Core loop:** pick a team → argue with evidence → play for points → everyone sees the answer at the same second → winners gloat.

Guest flow: open link → Team Boy / Team Girl → roster + badge → clues and quizzes → predictions and names → countdown lobby → synchronised reveal → winner cards → wishes → later, the same link becomes the birth announcement and settles the predictions.

| # | Mechanic | What the guest does | When | Release |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | تيم ولد / تيم بنت | Taps a side; sees who is on each team; live tug-of-war bar | Before | Launch |
| 2 | Synchronised reveal | Same countdown on every phone; answer appears for all at zero, including relatives abroad | During, live | Launch |
| 3 | Secret keeper | The doctor or a sister enters the answer by private link; the parents only see "sealed" | Before | Launch |
| 4 | Team badge | "أنا تيم ولد 💙" card with their name to post in the family group | Before | Launch |
| 5 | Old wives' tales | Parents answer the signs (cravings, bump shape); guests see the clues and may switch team once | Before | Launch |
| 6 | Choice of reveal | Host picks the reveal: balloon pop, scratch card, mystery box, wheel; each guest pops or scratches their own | During | Launch (2 styles), more later |
| 7 | Points battle | Quick rounds for team points: emoji puzzle, baby-item price guess, kids' song line | Before + during | Launch |
| 8 | "Who knows the parents?" quiz | Questions about mum and dad | Before | Launch |
| 9 | Name suggestions and vote | Suggests a name, upvotes others | Before / after | Launch |
| 10 | Prediction card | Birth date, weight, who the baby will look like; closest wins after the birth | Before → after birth | Launch |
| 11 | Winner moment | Winning team gets confetti and a "كنت عارف!" card; losing team gets a funny consolation card | During + after | Launch |
| 12 | Live room | Who is online, their city, reactions while waiting | During | Release 2 |
| 13 | Reaction capture | Records a selfie video or voice note of their reaction; parents get a wall | During + after | Release 2 |
| 14 | Baby bingo | Shuffled card for the in-person party | During | Release 2 |
| 15 | Wishes and du'a wall | A wish or advice for the parents | After | Launch |

**Egyptian touches:** badges as تيم إبريق vs تيم قُلّة (the old sebou symbols for boy and girl); a hon reveal — tap the brass mortar seven times and the seventh strike shows the colour; "اسمع كلام…" cards where guests write the instruction they'd shout at the baby; the same link later becomes the sebou invitation.

**Avoid:** any hint that one result is better — both outcomes get the same celebration, and losing-team cards are playful, not disappointed; betting language or money pools (points and titles only); the answer must never reach the phone before zero — not in the page data, the URL or the share image; keep pages private and unindexed, with a quiet postpone/cancel for the host.

## 5. Corporate events

**Core loop:** personal invite → assigned to a team (department or random "house") → earn entries and points before the day → live moments at the event → recap worth posting on LinkedIn.

| # | Mechanic | What the attendee does | When | Release |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Personalised invite | Opens to their name, title, company, table and team | Before | Launch |
| 2 | Teams | Department or random house; team page and running score | Before → after | Launch |
| 3 | Countdown with staged reveals | New teaser unlocks every few days: speaker, venue clue, product silhouette | Before | Launch |
| 4 | Predictions | Guess the product name, award winner, headcount; scored live | Before → during | Launch |
| 5 | Pre-event polls | Vote on topics, music, entertainment; result visibly shapes the event | Before | Launch |
| 6 | Award voting | People's choice, best team, best costume; live reveal | Before + during | Launch |
| 7 | Lucky draw (السحب) | Entries earned by RSVP, check-in and challenges; spin on the big screen | During | Launch (private events only — see below) |
| 8 | Live quiz with team mode | Company history, product, Ramadan fawazir | During, live | Release 2 |
| 9 | QR stamp passport | Collect stamps at booths, stations, branch corners | During | Release 2 |
| 10 | Q&A with upvoting | Ask the CEO, anonymously if they wish | During | Release 2 |
| 11 | Photo missions with live feed | Team photo challenges | During | Release 2 |
| 12 | Icebreaker card / "find someone who…" | A prompt per attendee; bingo of people to find | Before + during | Release 2 |
| 13 | Live word cloud / pulse | One word on the big screen | During | Release 2 |
| 14 | Contact swap | Scan someone's QR to exchange details and score | During + after | Release 2 |
| 15 | Recap + share card | Final scores, winners, photo wall, "I was on Team X, ranked #3" | After | Launch |

**Information blocks (launch):** date with doors-open and programme-start times, venue with map pin + hall/floor + gate + parking/valet, agenda with now/next marker, speakers and hosts with titles, dress code, plus-one policy, RSVP deadline, QR entry pass, food format, WhatsApp contact, prize rules. Ramadan: maghrib time, prayer area, programme start after eating. Store openings: opening offers and first-N perks.

**Egyptian touches:** a Ramadan iftar/suhoor mode (live countdown to maghrib, no games until after eating, calm page); a charity challenge where team points unlock donated meals; titles shown correctly (Dr., Eng.) and a VIP tier with reserved seating and no forced participation.

**Avoid:** individual public rankings that embarrass senior people; showing the full guest list where clients or competitors attend (organiser decides); music and loud games on iftar pages.

**Legal flag:** sources say prize promotions in Egypt need prior approval (Ministry of Social Solidarity; Consumer Protection Agency for consumer competitions). Whether a closed, free, employees-only draw is covered was not confirmed. The draw ships for private, invitation-only events; public draws (e.g. store openings) wait for legal advice.

## 6. What makes it spread

1. A personal share card after every action (badge, score, winner), in square and story formats, with the link inside.
2. A WhatsApp link preview that shows the live team score, so the link looks alive in the group chat.
3. A visible roster — "خالتو منى انضمت لتيم بنت" pulls the next relative in.
4. Average-based team scores, so the rivalry stays close.
5. Scheduled reasons to come back: daily question, countdown, prediction results, recap.
6. "Made with ⟨brand⟩" on the recap and every share card.

## 7. Placeholder brand and theming

Until you send the real name, logo and palette:

- **Placeholder name:** **لَمّة / Lamma** ("the gathering"). Not checked for trademarks or domains — it is a stand-in only.
- **Placeholder palette** (deliberately away from the maroon-and-gold every competitor uses):

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| `--brand-ink` | `#17131F` | Text, dark surfaces |
| `--brand-paper` | `#F7F2EA` | Light surfaces |
| `--brand-primary` | `#E4572E` | Buttons, key accents |
| `--brand-secondary` | `#1F8A7A` | Secondary accents, success |
| `--brand-highlight` | `#E9B44C` | Small highlights |

- **How the swap stays a one-file change:**
  - Brand name (Arabic and English), tagline, logo files and social links live in one config file.
  - Every colour in the product comes from the tokens above; no hard-coded brand colours anywhere.
  - Logo is a single component reading from the config.
  - Fonts are tokens too.
  - This applies to the platform's own surfaces (marketing site, dashboard, share-card footer). Invitation templates carry their own palettes, chosen by the customer, and do not change with the brand.

## 8. Scope check

Three occasion sets at launch, so the launch is built as: the play engine first, then each set as a content pack on top of it. The "Launch" column above is what ships first for each set; "Release 2" follows. The live, everyone-at-once features (synchronised reveal, big screen, live quiz rounds) need a real-time layer — the synchronised reveal is the only one that must be in the launch.

Template count at launch (my call): 2 wedding, 1 gender reveal, 1 corporate — four scenes to generate, each through the playbook's approval gates.
