# Event website / digital invitation platform — market research & feature brainstorm

Date: 2026-11-15. Web research only. Evidence labels: **[V]** read on the vendor's own page, **[S]** search snippet / third-party only, **[U]** unverified.

Known research gaps:
- Mithaq prices are hidden behind sign-up — not read.
- Instagram/TikTok can't be fetched, so the Instagram-first Egyptian sellers are under-covered.
- Designs were not visually reviewed (text fetch only), except Mithaq via the reference video.

---

## 1. Reference product: Mithaq (mithaq-invitation.com) [V]

Egyptian, looks like a one-person operation. Self-reported: 26.6K invitations created, 469K opens.

- **Events:** wedding, engagement, katb kitab + one "birthday gift" template.
- **Templates:** 13, each in Arabic and English, some premium.
- **Guest side:** wax-seal envelope opening (video-based in some), autoplay music, countdown, map button, calendar, photo album / flip gallery, scratch-to-reveal date, day programme, families section, dress code colours, Quran verse, RSVP, greetings book, guest voice greetings, guest photo upload.
- **Editor (their main strength):** click-to-edit text, per-element font/colour/size, drag/rotate/resize, music library or upload with trimmer, section toggles, phone/desktop preview, custom WhatsApp link-preview card, draft → publish.
- **Dashboard:** views, unique guests, attending/declined, traffic sources, devices, Excel export, printable QR, album + voice ZIP download.
- **Packages:** 4 one-time credit packs (edit window 30 / 60 / 90 days / lifetime), unlimited guests, link live for life, free tier without editor.
- **Payment:** fully manual — Vodafone Cash / InstaPay / bank transfer, upload receipt, admin activates.
- **Missing:** per-guest links, guest import, WhatsApp sending, reminders, QR check-in, money gifts/registry, custom domain, live stream, non-wedding occasions, card payments.

## 3. Arab region

Two separate product families exist, and nobody merges them:

**A. Page-style (like Mithaq)**
- Nfrah (Kuwait) — nfrah.com [V]: 15+ themes, cinematic intro, RSVP with dietary notes, guest album, guestbook, password. 3.1 / 8.9 KWD.
- Daawaty — daawaty.com [V]: 60 themes, many occasions incl. sebou, free plan, from $7.50/month (the only subscription seen).

**B. WhatsApp + QR guest operations (Saudi/Gulf)** — priced per invitation
- Mazeem — mazeem.app [V]: accept/decline buttons inside WhatsApp, delivered/read status per guest, QR only for those who accept, browser gate scanner, 10% free replacement invites. 1.9–3.8 SAR per invitation.
- Anwarat — anwarat.net [V]: QR issued after confirmation, cancelled on decline, single-use with duplicate alert, paced sending + "latest safe send date", guest data deleted after 72h. 0.9–2.3 SAR per guest.
- Da3wa Online — da3wa.online [V]: personalised card per guest, in-chat RSVP, reminders, import, single-use QR. $0.99–1.36 per guest, card payments.
- DINVITE, Kart (Apple Wallet pass), Invitek, Asfrt, Munasbat, Amsiyah, WASL [V]: same model.

## 4. Global — what the best do

- **US wedding sites (Zola, Joy, The Knot, Riley & Grey, Appy Couple):** the site is free; money comes from registry, cash-fund fees (0–2.95%), domains. Mature guest features: per-guest event visibility, household RSVP, plus-one limits, co-editors, guest photo slideshow.
- **Invitation platforms (Paperless Post, Greenvelope, Evite, Partiful, Luma):** envelope animations, open tracking, guest-count price bands, text blasts, waitlist/capacity, approval-gated registration, ticketing.
- **Indonesia (most mature link-invitation market):**
  - Katsudoto [V]: WhatsApp sending, pax limits, guest sessions, QR check-in with selfie, auto thank-you, table + souvenir tracking, digital envelope, live stream, Instagram filter, every feature sold à la carte.
  - Ini Undangan Kita [V]: **playable invitations** (mini-game, story-based themes built on how the couple met).
  - Datengdong [V]: tiers gate guest count, media count, active period, own domain.
- **Turkey:** Davetiyem [V] — cinematic scene opening built as a web scene (not a video), tap-to-play audio, static fallback. Davetlim [V] — WhatsApp delivery/read reports, auto reminder to non-responders, AI text in 4 tones, QR photo album, IBAN gift. Davetimde [V] — tiers by memory quota (photos / videos / voice notes) and how long the page lives.
- **Pakistan:** Invitewala [V] — one link, per-function RSVP, each family sees only its own functions, pay at publish.
- **Guest experience tools:** GuestCam [V] (QR photo upload, audio guestbook, live slideshow, selfie face search), POV [V] (disposable camera: shots per guest + delayed reveal), Wedibox [V] (find-your-seat), Kululu [V] (live photo wall with moderation), EventLive [V] (live stream with replay).
- **3D / WebGL:** no self-serve SaaS with real-time 3D invitations was found. Only a $50 Framer template (scroll-driven 3D rings ending on the RSVP button) and custom developer work. **This is an open gap.**
- **AI:** invitation text generation and face search exist; guest-facing AI concierge and AI voice/avatar invitations were not found.

## 5. Baseline — what we must have to be in the game

- One link opened from WhatsApp, mobile-first, no app, custom slug, custom WhatsApp link-preview card.
- Opening animation (envelope or equivalent), background music with tap-to-play.
- Names, date, venue, countdown, map (Google Maps), add-to-calendar.
- Gallery, story section, day programme, families section, dress code.
- RSVP with headcount/companions/children, greetings wall.
- Owner dashboard: replies, counts, Excel export, printable QR.
- Arabic + English, RTL-first.
- Self-serve editor with live preview (Mithaq sets the bar here).
- Guest photo upload and voice greetings (Mithaq, Farhetna, Nfrah already have these).
- Local payment (InstaPay / Vodafone Cash at minimum).

## 6. Gaps in the region (our opportunity)

1. **Hybrid product.** Egypt sells pretty pages with no guest operations; Saudi sells WhatsApp + QR with no page. Nobody does both well.
2. **Automated Egyptian checkout.** Every Egyptian player uses transfer + screenshot + human activation.
3. **Money gifts (no2ta) and registry.** No Arab player has it.
4. **Non-wedding occasions done properly.** Sebou, gender reveal, birthday, graduation, corporate are retitled wedding templates. No gender-reveal mechanic exists anywhere.
5. **Real-time 3D / scroll-driven experiences as a product.** Nobody.
6. **Live stream / video for relatives abroad.**
7. **Reminders, seating, plus-one and meal logic** on Egyptian products.
8. **Post-event value** — thank-you messages, memory page.
9. **Trust** — public EGP prices, permanent link, real company identity.
10. **Vendor channel** — halls, planners, photographers as resellers / white-label.
11. **Proper multi-page event website with custom domain.**

## 7. Proposed feature set

### 7.1 Experience (our headline differentiator)
- 3D scroll-driven scenes per occasion instead of a flat page; the scene is real-time web (not a video), so names/colours/photos are live data.
- Occasion-specific openings: envelope + wax seal (wedding), ring box (engagement), gift box / balloon (birthday), cradle / candles (sebou), sealed box (gender reveal), stage curtain / badge (corporate, graduation).
- Device-tier detection, lightweight fallback for low-end Android, reduced-motion variant, skip-intro.
- Guest name on the opening ("Dear Ahmed & family") via per-guest links.
- Guest-switchable language.

### 7.2 Ideas nobody has
- **Synchronised gender reveal:** guests vote boy/girl beforehand, live vote tally, then the page reveals for everyone at the same moment (host-triggered or timed), with replay.
- **No2ta ledger:** InstaPay / wallet gift details on the page + a private record for the hosts of who gave what — the digital version of the no2ta notebook families already keep to return gifts later.
- **Event-day live mode:** the same link changes state — before (invitation + RSVP), during (live schedule "now", live photo wall, live stream, find-your-seat), after (album, thank-you notes, memory page).
- **One link, many functions:** engagement, henna, katb kitab, wedding — per-guest visibility and per-function RSVP; groom-side / bride-side guest ownership.
- **Time capsule:** guests leave text/voice/video messages that unlock on the first anniversary or the child's birthday.
- **Sebou-specific interactions:** baby name reveal, tap-along traditional sebou rituals, wishes for the baby.
- **Birthday interactions:** blow the candles (microphone), wish jar, group gift.
- **Guest games:** "who knows the couple best" quiz with leaderboard, song requests / playlist voting for the DJ.
- **AI:** invitation text in Egyptian dialect / formal Arabic / English in several tones; guest-facing concierge that answers "where do I park / what time / dress code" from the event data; face search across the event album.
- **Photographer handoff:** the event photographer uploads the professional gallery to the same link; guests find their photos by selfie.

### 7.3 Guest operations (the Gulf model, brought to Egypt)
- Guest list import (Excel / contacts), groups, tags, pax caps.
- Per-guest link and QR entry pass; confirmation-gated, single-use, duplicate-scan alert.
- Browser-based gate scanner with multiple gate accounts; live arrivals counter.
- WhatsApp sending from the dashboard with delivered/read status, reminders to non-responders only, auto thank-you after the event.
- Seating/table assignment and find-your-seat.
- Capacity, waitlist, replacement invitations.

### 7.4 Corporate
- Logo and brand colours, agenda and speakers, registration form with approval, QR badges, check-in, CSV export, cohost roles, custom domain.

### 7.5 Business / platform
- Automated checkout (cards + wallets + Fawry through a local gateway) — gateway choice to be verified.
- Public EGP pricing, permanent link.
- Reseller / white-label accounts for halls, planners, photographers.
- Physical add-ons: printed QR cards for tables, NFC cards.
- Pricing levers seen elsewhere: template class, guest cap, media quota, page lifetime, per-invitation WhatsApp sending, done-for-you service tier, express delivery.

### Pricing note
The whole region sells **one-time per event**, not subscriptions (only Daawaty has a monthly plan). A monthly subscription makes sense for the vendor/reseller side, not for a couple with one wedding.

---

## 8. Open decisions

1. Self-serve SaaS, done-for-you studio, or both (same package sold two ways)?
2. Launch scope: which occasions first, and is guest operations (WhatsApp/QR) in v1 or v2?
3. WhatsApp sending: official WhatsApp Business API (per-message cost, template approval) vs. share links only at launch.
4. Tech stack for 3D scenes and the editor.
5. Payment gateway.
6. Brand name and Arabic/English positioning.
